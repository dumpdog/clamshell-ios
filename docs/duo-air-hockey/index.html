Duo Air Hockey

Real air hockey across the fold of your iPhone Duo. Lay it flat and fully open, and the fold becomes the center line.

Help

The rink doesn't line up with the fold. Open the Duo all the way and lay it flat before starting a match. The rink locks its orientation once a match begins.

I bought Pro on another device. Tap the yellow unlock text on the main screen, then Restore Purchase.

Does it work on other iPhones? Yes. The rink runs along the long side of the screen, with the center line in the middle.
