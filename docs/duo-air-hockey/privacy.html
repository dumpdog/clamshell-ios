Duo Air Hockey Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Duo Air Hockey is an air hockey game for iPhone. It doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. This page explains the details.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere. It never asks for your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few settings on your iPhone so it works the way you left it: whether you've unlocked Pro, and how many free two-player matches you've played today. This information never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and the app only receives confirmation that the purchase was made. We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
