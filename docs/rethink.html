Rethink · iPhone Duo · 25 Oct 2026

Keep the games. Stop closing the phone.

Folding the whole phone shut to play needs two hands, takes about a second per move, and puts strain on a $1,999 hinge. The fold is still the most interesting thing about the Duo, so the fix is to change how you hold it, not to throw the idea away.

Recommendation: Clamshell Handheld.

Hold the Duo open sideways, like a Nintendo DS. The game plays on the top half, your thumbs tap the bottom half, and the hinge only moves in small tilts inside a comfortable range. You never go below about 95°. It reuses all of the existing engine, and Fold Pet can join later as the mascot.

The comfort band

Every hinge game now reads its angle from this band only. "Squeeze" means tilting the top half toward you a little, never folding it shut.

0° closed95°165°180° flat

Below 95°: old Clamshell territory, now avoided. 95° to 165°: the play band. These numbers are a first guess and live in one constant to tune on your Duo.

Handheld and Fold Pet are close. Handheld wins because it ships from code you already have running, and its clip still reads in a second ("my phone is a Nintendo DS"). Fold Pet is the most comfortable idea on the list, but it's a new app that spreads slowly. Tent and two-screen ideas are ruled out: a book fold can't bend backwards into a tent, and apps can only use the outer screen while open through a camera accessory.

What changes in the four games

Thumb tap

Chomp

Snap the phone shut. Tap when the snack is between the jaws.

Alternate thumbs

Balloon

Flap the fold. Tap left, right, left, right to pump.

Small hinge tilt

Drawbridge

Same game, but the target angle sits inside the comfort band.

Slow hinge tilt

Sneak

Close the book first. Squeeze it in a little, then open it slowly.

Try this before trusting the scores

  • Hold the Duo open sideways for three minutes with your thumbs on the bottom half. Does it get heavy?
  • Tilt the top half 20° toward you and back ten times. Is that easy, or does it need a second hand?
  • If tilting feels bad, the games can drop to thumbs only and the hinge just picks the mode.
